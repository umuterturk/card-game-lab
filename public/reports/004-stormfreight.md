# Stormfreight — Experiment 004

> **Abridged archival summary.** The complete long-form report for this early experiment has not been imported to the repository. Values below are taken from the original experiment summary.

**Status:** PASS  
**Qualitative design score:** 7.9/10

## Concept

Load cargo ships, assign valuable cards as shields, and risk the entire shipment for ×3 rewards.

## Components

24 resource cards (1–6); 14 storm cards (1–7); two ships.

## Gameplay loop

Load up to three cargo cards and one shield per ship. Bank the cargo product or challenge a storm.

## Risk/reward

A storm succeeds if storm value + previous successes ≤ shield; success triples, failure destroys the shipment.

## Review finding

Strong emotional loop; shield allocation creates an opportunity cost.

## Simulation evidence (historical)

Strategic averaged 505 vs conservative 373 and random 203.

**Limitations:** The reported results are from earlier automated experiments. Human enjoyment and commercial originality remain unverified.
