# Rebound — Experiment 008

> **Abridged archival summary.** The complete long-form report for this early experiment has not been imported to the repository. Values below are taken from the original experiment summary.

**Status:** REVISE  
**Qualitative design score:** 6.9/10

## Concept

Use positive and negative cards to manage a meter, then cross a danger line and hope to return safely.

## Components

24 signed cards; one meter from −10 to +10.

## Gameplay loop

Place signed values in the safe zone (−6 to +6), create reversal combos, or Bank.

## Risk/reward

Overload moves outside the safe zone then reveals a correction; success triples, failure salvages one quarter.

## Review finding

A few favorable draws dominate outcomes.

## Simulation evidence (historical)

Adaptive averaged 516 vs safe 269 but won only 55% of matched runs.

**Limitations:** The reported results are from earlier automated experiments. Human enjoyment and commercial originality remain unverified.
