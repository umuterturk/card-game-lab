# Switchback — Experiment 006

> **Abridged archival summary.** The complete long-form report for this early experiment has not been imported to the repository. Values below are taken from the original experiment summary.

**Status:** PASS  
**Qualitative design score:** 7.5/10

## Concept

Build alternating high-low movements along a track and gamble on unseen reversals.

## Components

24 numbered cards; one numerical track; three-card hand.

## Gameplay loop

Place numbers to alternate the movement direction and grow a combo up to ×8. Bank the pot or attempt Blind Turn.

## Risk/reward

Reversing unseen card doubles the pot; failing loses it. Two successful blind turns trigger an auto-bank.

## Review finding

Card counting can make some blind turns guaranteed.

## Simulation evidence (historical)

Adaptive averaged 1,074 vs no-blind strategic 479.

**Limitations:** The reported results are from earlier automated experiments. Human enjoyment and commercial originality remain unverified.
