# Stepwire — Experiment 002

> **Abridged archival summary.** The complete long-form report for this early experiment has not been imported to the repository. Values below are taken from the original experiment summary.

**Status:** PASS  
**Qualitative design score:** 7.5/10

## Concept

Extend two numerical chains from either end, shaping the odds of a profitable blind finish.

## Components

24 numbered cards; two five-card chains; three-card hand.

## Gameplay loop

Place a number on either end if it differs from its neighbor by one; bank a chain or blind-complete it.

## Risk/reward

A matching unseen fifth card earns double payout; a non-fitting card burns the chain.

## Review finding

The same multiset of cards creates different future odds depending on order.

## Simulation evidence (historical)

Strategic averaged 374 vs random 168 over 1,000 seeds.

**Limitations:** The reported results are from earlier automated experiments. Human enjoyment and commercial originality remain unverified.
