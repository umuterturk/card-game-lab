# Twinlock — Experiment 001

> **Abridged archival summary.** The complete long-form report for this early experiment has not been imported to the repository. Values below are taken from the original experiment summary.

**Status:** PASS  
**Qualitative design score:** 7.9/10

## Concept

Balance two-sided vaults, then decide whether to secure the payout or draw a blind fourth card.

## Components

24 numbered cards; three vaults; three-card hand.

## Gameplay loop

Place cards on either side of a vault; the closer the sums, the higher the payout. Cash out early or blind-finish a three-card vault.

## Risk/reward

Blind completion doubles the payout if totals are within one; otherwise the vault burns.

## Review finding

Small decision space but clear risk and board manipulation.

## Simulation evidence (historical)

Strategy averaged 962 vs random 152 in 1,000 matched-seed runs.

**Limitations:** The reported results are from earlier automated experiments. Human enjoyment and commercial originality remain unverified.
