# Tenfold — Experiment 003

> **Abridged archival summary.** The complete long-form report for this early experiment has not been imported to the repository. Values below are taken from the original experiment summary.

**Status:** REVISE  
**Qualitative design score:** 7.25/10

## Concept

Arrange a 3×3 grid to create scoring lines that total ten, then risk your grid on a hidden Seal.

## Components

3×3 grid; numbered deck and three-card hand.

## Gameplay loop

Arrange numbers so rows, columns, and diagonals produce exact-ten sums; secure points or attempt a Blind Seal.

## Risk/reward

Blind Seal multiplies new line rewards but can destroy accumulated grid value.

## Review finding

Huge jackpots overshadow ordinary placement decisions.

## Simulation evidence (historical)

Adaptive averaged 397 vs aggressive 376 in the original version; distinction was uncertain.

**Limitations:** The reported results are from earlier automated experiments. Human enjoyment and commercial originality remain unverified.
