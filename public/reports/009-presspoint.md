# Presspoint — Experiment 009

> **Abridged archival summary.** The complete long-form report for this early experiment has not been imported to the repository. Values below are taken from the original experiment summary.

**Status:** PASS  
**Qualitative design score:** 7.25/10

## Concept

Manipulate two threshold guards around a center card to control scoring and gambling odds.

## Components

24 numbered cards; left guard, center piston, right guard; four Press Chips.

## Gameplay loop

Replace machine cards and score when the center is above or below both guards; alternate patterns for combo points.

## Risk/reward

Blind Press stakes half or all of a pot on the center switching to the opposite pattern.

## Review finding

Guard adjustments change both possible rewards and probability.

## Simulation evidence (historical)

Adaptive averaged 352 vs safe 188; banking was rarely used.

**Limitations:** The reported results are from earlier automated experiments. Human enjoyment and commercial originality remain unverified.
