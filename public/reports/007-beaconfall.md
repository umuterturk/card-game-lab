# Beaconfall — Experiment 007

> **Abridged archival summary.** The complete long-form report for this early experiment has not been imported to the repository. Values below are taken from the original experiment summary.

**Status:** PASS  
**Qualitative design score:** 7.6/10

## Concept

Navigate a circular board to land on scoring Beacons and manage scarce banking opportunities.

## Components

24 numbered cards; 7-position ring; four Bank Seals.

## Gameplay loop

Play a movement card clockwise, score Beacon landings, and build a combo. Bank or Surge.

## Risk/reward

Unseen Surge movement doubles an eligible Beacon payout; a miss destroys the pot.

## Review finding

Positions change which card values are useful.

## Simulation evidence (historical)

Bold averaged 219 vs safe planner 131.

**Limitations:** The reported results are from earlier automated experiments. Human enjoyment and commercial originality remain unverified.
