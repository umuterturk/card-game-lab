# Cinderloop — Experiment 005

> **Abridged archival summary.** The complete long-form report for this early experiment has not been imported to the repository. Values below are taken from the original experiment summary.

**Status:** REVISE  
**Qualitative design score:** 6.6/10

## Concept

Feed and cool a furnace to reopen risky opportunities for exponential rewards.

## Components

24 numbered cards; two furnaces with heat cap 12.

## Gameplay loop

Feed cards to raise heat, Vent to reduce it, and Bank a furnace at its current multiplier.

## Risk/reward

Stoke draws an unseen number when heat is 9–11: stay ≤12 to multiply; otherwise burn.

## Review finding

Extreme variance makes skilled average returns unreliable per run.

## Simulation evidence (historical)

Strategic mean 250 vs conservative 155, but won fewer than half matched games.

**Limitations:** The reported results are from earlier automated experiments. Human enjoyment and commercial originality remain unverified.
